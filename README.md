# Healthcare Diabetes Analysis 🏥

## Project Overview
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Pima Indians Diabetes Dataset 
to identify key risk factors that contribute to diabetes.

## Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle — Pima Indians Diabetes Dataset
- **Patients:** 768 women aged 21 and above
- **Features:** 9 columns including Glucose, BMI, Age, Insulin
- **Target:** Outcome (0 = Non-Diabetic, 1 = Diabetic)

## Tools Used
- **Python** — Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Google Colab** — Development environment

## Key Findings

| # | Finding | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overall Diabetes Rate | 34.9% — 3x higher than global average |
| 2 | Glucose — Diabetic vs Non-Diabetic | 142.3 vs 110.6 — 29% higher |
| 3 | BMI — Diabetic vs Non-Diabetic | 35.39 vs 30.84 — 15% higher |
| 4 | Age — Diabetic vs Non-Diabetic | 37.07 vs 31.19 — 19% higher |
| 5 | Strongest Predictor | Glucose (correlation 0.50) |
| 6 | Top 3 Risk Factors | Glucose → Insulin → BMI |

## Visualizations

## Recommendations
- Early diabetes screening should prioritise Glucose and Insulin testing
- Weight management programs can significantly reduce diabetes risk
- Women above 35 years should get regular diabetes checkups
- Pima Indian community needs targeted healthcare intervention

## Conclusion
Glucose level is the strongest predictor of diabetes (correlation 0.50) 
followed by Insulin (0.38) and BMI (0.32). Early detection focusing 
on these key risk factors can significantly improve patient outcomes.
